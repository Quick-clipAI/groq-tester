How to read the results
  • Works: answered in under 30 s and (for research) searched.
  • Works but slow: answered, but the app stops waiting after 30 s (Short) or 50 s (Deep), so the app sees a failure.
  • No search: answered but did not browse. The app would get an answer with no sources.
  • Limit reached / Not available / Key problem: Groq refused. The "Full reply" shows Groq's own words.
  • If the site test fails but the direct test works, the fault is on Vercel (timeout or proxy). If both fail the same way, it is Groq or the key.
